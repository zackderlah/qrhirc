# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Health professionals, researchers, collaborating organisations, and community members in Darling Downs and South West Queensland. The user requested a redesign based on the completed research report.

## Product Purpose
Queensland Rural Health Innovation and Research Collaborative brings hospitals, health services, universities, primary care and business together to improve regional, rural and remote health through research and workforce development.

## Capabilities and Constraints
This delivery redesigns the landing page and provides several working visual iterations. Research, training, membership and contact destinations can link to the existing verified website. Do not fabricate research outcomes, personnel, clinical services, statistics, or upcoming event dates. Preview work is not approval to replace the client's production website.

## Brand Commitments
Preserve QRHIRC's name, factual regional remit and Acknowledgement of Country. Completely overhaul the visual style using the earlier Mobbin reference synthesis: verifiable expertise, task clarity, structured training and human community impact.

## Evidence on Hand
https://www.qrhirc.com/ ; /about-us ; /research-priorities ; /events ; /contact-us. Existing site names member organisations, funded research projects and a 2025 Impact Report. IRTP is a free five-month mentor-supported in-person programme with four full-day workshops. Availability of the next intake is by enquiry.

## Product Principles
- Show specific work and sources before broad claims.
- Keep community participation welcoming and plain-language.
- Make research and training easy to discover.
- Preserve approved factual content and distinguish ambitions from results.

## Open decisions
Final visual selection remains with the user after live preview. No client brand guide or new photography supplied.
